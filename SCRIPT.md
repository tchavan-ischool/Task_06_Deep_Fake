# Approach 1 — ElevenLabs Synthetic Audio

## Final Source Script

This is an AI-generated synthetic voice created for an academic research project.

We often hear large numbers used to describe social-media activity, but averages do not always represent a typical post.

For this project, I analyzed three datasets associated with the 2024 presidential election: Facebook advertisements, Facebook posts, and Twitter/X posts.

Together, the datasets contain two hundred ninety-three thousand, fifty-eight records.

The Facebook advertising dataset contains two hundred forty-six thousand, seven hundred forty-five advertisements.

Estimated advertising spending across these records totals approximately two hundred sixty-one point nine million dollars, with approximately eleven point two five billion estimated impressions.

But an especially interesting pattern appears in social-media engagement.

For Facebook posts with numeric interaction data, the median was only one hundred thirty-three interactions, while the mean was approximately two thousand, two hundred ten.

Twitter/X showed a similar pattern.

Across twenty-seven thousand, three hundred four posts, the median number of likes was one thousand, four hundred six, while the mean was approximately six thousand, nine hundred fourteen.

The difference matters because a relatively small number of highly engaged posts can dramatically increase the average.

This experiment also demonstrates an important lesson about artificial intelligence.

AI can summarize statistics and present information convincingly, but realistic presentation does not guarantee accuracy.

The underlying data still needs to be verified by humans.

As synthetic media becomes increasingly realistic, transparent disclosure, accurate sourcing, and human verification become increasingly important.

This presentation was created using artificial intelligence for educational research.

No real person's identity or voice was represented.

# Script Design

The scripts deliberately contain explicit disclosure that the media is synthetic.

The ElevenLabs script was longer because the audio-only format allowed the research findings to be explained in greater detail.

The Vidnoz script was shortened because of limitations associated with the free generation tier.

Version 2 of the Vidnoz script intentionally used shorter sentences and stronger sentence boundaries to test whether additional pauses would make the synthetic avatar appear more natural.
